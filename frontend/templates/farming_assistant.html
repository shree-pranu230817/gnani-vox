Supported Agricultural Knowledge Domains

Every answer follows ICAR agronomy guidelines and university package of practices (TNAU, UAS, PJTSAU, PAU).

🌱

Crop Cultivation & Management

Spacing, sowing methods, nursery management, transplanting, and lifecycle agronomy for all major crops.

🌾

Seed Selection & Varieties

High-yielding certified varieties, drought/flood-tolerant hybrids, seed treatment rates, and germination tests.

🧪

Fertilizers & Nutrients

Basal and split top-dressing schedules for Urea, DAP, MOP, Zinc, Boron, and micronutrient deficiency cures.

💧

Irrigation & Water Management

Drip emitter spacing, alternate wetting and drying (AWD), sprinkler timing, and critical moisture growth stages.

🟫

Soil Health & Preparation

Deep summer ploughing, green manure crops (dhaincha, sunnhemp), soil pH correction, and organic carbon enrichment.

🦠

Crop Diseases & Diagnosis

Bacterial leaf blight, sheath rot, blast, powdery mildew, wilt, and viral mosaic symptom differentiation.

🐛

Pest Management (IPM)

Pheromone traps, light traps, neem oil biocontrol, Trichogramma cards, and approved selective pesticides.

🌿

Organic Farming Inputs

Step-by-step preparation of Jeevamrutha, Ghana Jeevamrutha, Panchagavya, Beejamrutha, and Agniastra.

🌾

Weed Management

Cono-weeder mechanical weeding, mulching, pre-emergence and post-emergence herbicide safety guidelines.

📦

Harvesting & Storage

Grain moisture benchmarks (12-14%), hermetic storage bags (PICS), cold storage tips, and warehouse protection.

⛅

Weather Precautions

Unseasonal cyclonic rainfall drainage, heat wave mulching, frost protection, and hail damage mitigation.

🚜

Agri Equipment & Machinery

Custom Hiring Centers (CHC), power tillers, seed drills, drone spraying protocols, and laser land levellers.

♻️

Sustainable Farming

Legume crop rotation, zero tillage, agro-forestry, solar pump integration, and farm pond rainwater harvesting.
