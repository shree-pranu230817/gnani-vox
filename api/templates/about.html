GNANI VOX

One Voice. Many Languages. No Barriers.

AI-Powered Communication and Agricultural Guidance Platform

GNANI VOX connects people through intelligent multilingual voice translation and provides farmers with accessible AI-powered agricultural guidance. Built for Indian communities — farmers, workers, students, travellers and shopkeepers — who face language barriers every day.

🤝 Powered by Gnani AI — Prisma STT · Timbre TTS · Evon NLP
🌐
Feature 1

VoxConnect

Speak in Your Language. Understand Any Language.

VoxConnect is a universal voice translator designed for everyone: farmers, daily-wage workers, students, travellers, shopkeepers and business owners. Any two people speaking different languages can communicate naturally through real-time voice translation.

  • Speak naturally — Gnani Prisma transcribes your speech instantly
  • Gemini AI translates to the target language accurately
  • Gnani Timbre speaks the translation in a natural voice
  • Conversation Mode: two-person live dialogue with real-time switching
  • Supports 10+ Indian languages including Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam and more

Feature 2

KrishiGuide AI

Your Intelligent Agricultural Guidance Assistant.

KrishiGuide AI is a dedicated farming assistant that answers all agricultural questions through voice or text — with permanent conversation memory. It remembers your crop name, growth stage, symptoms and soil type across all follow-up questions.

  • Ask about crops, fertilizers, pests, diseases, irrigation and soil
  • AI remembers the full conversation — no need to repeat yourself
  • Upload a crop photo for visual disease diagnosis
  • Receive answers in your chosen Indian language
  • Powered by Gemini AI with ICAR agricultural knowledge grounding

How GNANI VOX Works

Three Gnani AI services working together in real-time

1
🎙️

Speech Recognition

Gnani Prisma STT converts your spoken words into text with high accuracy, even for Indian language accents and dialects.

2
🧠

AI Translation & Reasoning

Gemini AI translates between languages and answers farming questions using ICAR-grounded agricultural knowledge.

3
🔊

Voice Synthesis

Gnani Timbre TTS converts the translated or AI-generated answer into natural, clear speech in your chosen language.

4
🗄️

Persistent Memory

All conversations are stored in a local SQLite database so KrishiGuide AI remembers every detail across follow-up questions.
